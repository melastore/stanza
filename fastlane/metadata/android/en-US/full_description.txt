Stanza is a Pomodoro and interval timer with a frosted glass look.

Each focus session is called a stanza. A cycle is a few stanzas with short breaks between them and a long break at the end.

Features:
• Focus, short break and long break timers with adjustable lengths
• Countdown in the notification and on the lock screen, drawn by the system so the phone doesn't wake every second
• Exact alarms, so sessions end on time even in Doze
• Keeps time through reboots and the app being closed
• Tasks with estimated and completed stanzas
• Stats with daily and weekly totals, streaks and a yearly heatmap
• Several timer layouts and colour themes
• Quick Settings tile and home screen widget
• "Reduce Transparency" setting that swaps the blur for solid surfaces
• No accounts, no internet, no tracking, no ads
